1. Program scope

The program will provide one-to-one replies to BarnWorks customer inquiries, including project questions, quote requests, proof discussions, and order questions. Relevant quote, proof, and order-status follow-ups require consent to this customer-support program and stay within the customer’s support topics.

No marketing, bulk, personal, or group messages are included in this campaign.

2. Participation and consent

At launch, text START to (445) 275-7628 after reviewing the program disclosure to consent to AI-assisted BarnWorks support for your inquiries, quotes, proofs, and orders, including relevant follow-ups. Participation is optional and is not a condition of purchase.

An ordinary customer inquiry without START permits relevant replies within that exchange only; it does not enroll you in recurring follow-ups. Existing customer status, a purchase, reading this website, or emailing support does not itself provide consent to proactive SMS messages. A different message purpose requires separate consent.

3. Frequency, charges, and delivery

Message frequency varies with your inquiries and consented project or order follow-ups. Message and data rates may apply. Carrier charges depend on your mobile plan. Any separate program charges will be disclosed before participation.

SMS can be delayed or fail. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Do not rely on the line for emergencies or critical, time-sensitive instructions.

4. Opting out and getting help

When SMS launches, reply STOP to unsubscribe. Opt-out will suppress future program messages and cancel pending support replies and follow-ups. At most one final confirmation will be sent.

After reviewing the program disclosure, text START again to request re-enrollment. An arbitrary new inquiry will not re-enroll you, and canceled prior drafts will not be revived.

Reply HELP for program information when the service is available, or email iam@evankrishnakumar.com. Before SMS launch, use email for support questions.

5. AI assistance and owner review

Replies will be AI-assisted and reviewed by Evan before dispatch. AI output may be incorrect, incomplete, or outdated. Verify project and order details before relying on them.

Pricing, contracts, approvals, order acceptance or changes, and other consequential commitments require Evan’s approval. An AI draft does not independently commit BarnWorks to a price, delivery date, order change, or contract.

Use the line for BarnWorks customer support. Do not use it for unlawful activity, harassment, impersonation, unsolicited messaging, or sharing another person’s private information without permission. Evan may limit or pause the service for maintenance, abuse prevention, or provider and carrier requirements. Availability and message delivery are not guaranteed.

6. Privacy and updates

Read the Privacy Policy for information about phone numbers, message content, consent records, customer-support details, service providers, and retention.

Material changes to message purpose or program scope will be disclosed, and additional consent obtained where required. Updated terms will be posted here with an effective date.
