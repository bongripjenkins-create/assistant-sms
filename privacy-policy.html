BarnWorks customer SMS support

Privacy Policy

Information about the BarnWorks customer-support website and planned AI-assisted SMS program, operated by Evan Krishnakumar.

Effective October 2, 2026 · SMS service is pre-launch

This policy covers this informational website and BarnWorks customer SMS support at +1 445 275 7628. The website is available now. The SMS practices below describe the planned program and will apply when it launches. The SMS integration is not yet live.

1. Information involved

The planned SMS service will process your phone number; messages you send and receive; message timestamps, identifiers, and delivery status; consent and opt-out records; and project, quote, proof, or order details you provide or authorize for a support exchange.

Evan will review relevant customer messages using an OpenAI-powered assistant to prepare replies, review the recipient and exact text, and approve dispatch. OpenAI processes the information included in that assistant conversation. This is an owner-assisted workflow; SMS traffic is not automatically connected to an always-on assistant.

The intended drafting context is limited to your support exchange and approved BarnWorks instructions. Unrelated private conversations, other customers’ information, email, or files are not included automatically. Avoid sending passwords, authentication codes, payment details, or sensitive personal information about yourself or others.

2. How information is used

Information will be used to respond to your BarnWorks inquiries, prepare and review AI-assisted replies, manage consented quote, proof, and order-status follow-ups, record messaging consent, honor opt-outs, resolve support requests, and operate and troubleshoot the service.

Evan can access local support messages, drafts, and consent records and review the content brought into AI-assisted drafting. Message frequency varies with your inquiries and consented project or order follow-ups. Message and data rates may apply.

3. Mobile information and service providers

Mobile numbers, SMS opt-in data, and consent will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS consent will not be transferred to another sender.

Twilio and telecommunications carriers will process phone numbers, message content, and delivery information to transmit SMS messages. OpenAI will process the customer information brought into the owner-assisted drafting conversation. Eligible support messages, drafts, and consent records will be stored locally on Evan’s computer. These operational uses are distinct from sharing information for third-party marketing.

Provider-side use and retention depend on each provider’s applicable policies and account settings. This policy does not promise that content is excluded from model training, encrypted end-to-end, or inaccessible to those providers. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

4. Retention, deletion, and access

At SMS launch, our local retention target is 30 days for support message and reply-draft content. Retention controls for this target will be enabled before live SMS processing begins. No automatic cleanup is represented as currently running for a live SMS service.

Consent evidence will be retained while participation is active and afterward as needed to demonstrate consent and meet applicable record requirements. Opt-out suppression information will be retained until valid re-enrollment or final service shutdown, subject to applicable record requirements.

The local rule covers active local message and draft storage. Provider-side records and OpenAI conversation history follow their own policies and settings. Local cleanup does not delete Twilio or OpenAI records or guarantee erasure from backups or filesystem remnants.

Contact iam@evankrishnakumar.com with privacy questions or requests about your information. Identity verification and record requirements may affect how a request can be handled. No SMS DELETE feature, immediate deletion guarantee, or provider-deletion deadline is offered.

5. Your messaging choices

At launch, text START after reviewing the messaging consent disclosure to consent to BarnWorks customer support for your inquiries, quotes, proofs, and orders, including relevant follow-ups. An ordinary inquiry without START permits relevant replies in that exchange only. A different message purpose requires separate consent.

Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for program information when the SMS service is available. Opt-out will suppress future program messages and cancel pending support replies and follow-ups; at most one final confirmation will be sent. An arbitrary new inquiry will not re-enroll you. After reviewing the disclosure, START requests re-enrollment without reviving old canceled drafts.

Unsubscribing does not itself delete records needed to meet applicable requirements or honor your opt-out. Until SMS launch, use iam@evankrishnakumar.com for program questions.

6. The informational website

This website has no signup form, analytics code, advertising scripts, embedded third-party content, or application cookies. Visiting it does not enroll you in SMS messages.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub’s Pages data-collection documentation and the GitHub Privacy Statement. GitHub Pages hosts these informational pages; SMS processing uses a separate service.
